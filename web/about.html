Paint 10: a free MS Paint alternative, online

Paint 10 recreates the Windows 10-era Microsoft Paint interface in your web browser, with its ribbon, brushes, shapes, and familiar shortcuts. It is free, open source, and requires no account or installation.

Open Paint 10

Draw, paste, and edit pictures

Start with a blank canvas, open an image, or paste from your clipboard. Use the pencil, fill bucket, eraser, nine brushes, and 23 shapes. Select, crop, resize, flip, or rotate images; the canvas grows to fit large pasted images and rotated selections.

Classic Paint, with a few extras

Add text you can edit again later, arrange optional layers, work with transparent backgrounds, or zoom in for pixel art. The sun / Auto / moon switch chooses light mode, your system theme, or dark mode and remembers your choice. Touchpad pinch and Ctrl+wheel zoom the picture.

Local files, no uploads

Your pictures are processed on your device. Paint 10 has no image upload service or analytics. Save a PNG or another image format to share your work, or a .p10 project to keep layers and text editable. Browser saves download a file; download your work before closing or reloading, because pictures are not autosaved.

Browser and desktop

The web app needs a current desktop browser with JavaScript, WebAssembly, and WebGL. Native builds are also available for Linux, Windows, and macOS. See the setup instructions and shortcuts or the browser guide.
