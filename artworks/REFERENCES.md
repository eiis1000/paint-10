# Visual and subject references

These references are viewed separately while drawing. The finished pictures
are made inside Paint 10; reference pixels are not imported into them.

- **Mona Lisa:** the [Louvre's description](https://www.louvre.fr/en/explore/the-palace/from-the-mona-lisa-to-the-wedding-feast-at-cana)
  and the [public-domain C2RMF reproduction](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mona_Lisa,_by_Leonardo_da_Vinci,_from_C2RMF_retouched.jpg).
  The reproduction was reduced for inspection only; it was not opened in Paint 10.
- **AirSense 10 and AirFit P30i:** [ResMed's machine page](https://www.resmed.com/en-us/products/cpap/machines/airsense-10/),
  [ResMed's mask guide](https://maskguide.resmed.com/airfit-p30i), and the
  [CPAP.com product photograph](https://www.cpap.com/products/airsense-10-p30i-mask-bundle).
  The photo shows the black control panel, silver dial, side humidifier chamber,
  top hose connection, white mask frame, blue headgear, and paired nasal pillows.
- **Ant-colony slide:** [Marco Dorigo's Scholarpedia article](https://www.scholarpedia.org/article/Ant_colony_optimization)
  describes pheromone reinforcement and evaporation in ant-colony optimization.
  The planned slide will distinguish an illustrative simulation from measured
  results; no invented simulation outcomes will be presented as data.
